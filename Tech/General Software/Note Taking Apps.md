
1. Google Keep
	Quick and robust, no sync issues. The only downside is lack of organization, there are labels, but thats it. So this lack of organizational tools is one reason that I had to look for other services.

2. Microsoft Onenote
	Could have been the best service here, but the sync issues mean that I have to always use OneNote with utmost care. I cant expect two instances of onenote being active and not causing issues with sync. Moreover, these issues can only be sorted via the desktop site or the windows app. Basically a big fuck you to all the other platforms. If I am just with my android device, then good luck if any sync issue arrives. Also, these sync issues have now caused me data loss for almost 7-8 different times over the past 5 years. Initially, after those issues happened, I would just drop onenote. But now I just keep my backups and use onenote extremely carefully. This fickleness with onenote is the very reason I started using Notion. First two comments here: https://www.reddit.com/r/OneNote/comments/m81d6m/alternative_for_onenote/

3. Dropbox paper
	Awful loading time for the website. Sometimes, it just doesnt load.

4. Notion
	It is pathetic that such a wonderful combination of Google keep’s robustness and Onenote’s organization is all wasted because Notion’s team couldnt come up with a decent android app. You can nest pages into one another, create sections, tables, embed videos documents and what not as much as you want but its of no use if all of this just stays like eye-candy on your mobile. Even without all those heavy attachments or files, even with a basic text file, the notion app is quite slow and heavy. I can do the basic editing or writing a bit on the mobile site or app, heck even currently I am writing these reviews from my android device. However, I am just writing, the formatting has taken a toll here with multiple number 1 in this list and no way to reorganize them without opening it on my laptop. So, due to the lack of a good android app, I still have to stick with Gkeep for taking notes quickly on the go on my phone.

5. Evernote
	A two device limit for the free account where each web login also counts towards that limit meant that I will have to find better free alternatives.

6. Word document
	No sync issues when using Google docs, very robust, android app not as great as GKeep but way better than Notion. The only issue that I come across here is the lack of strcuture. You have to nest the folders and files yourself and even after that searching through the files can be a bit of an issue, not to forget that each page will open in an instance of its own in Google docs, so that might cause a few problems when navigating on mobile but overall, turning a blind eye to the lack of organization, google docs is good.

7. Standard notes
	Free for basic notes, no body can read, has encryption better than AES256, company is privacy focused and doesnt look at user data, only has basic metadata for prevention of resource abuse by bots. Company claims on page that you can even store passwords in it without fear. Free plan is good enough for you, premium ones make the product from a google keep copy to a more notion-esque thing.

8. Joplin
	No web client, has android, windows, ubuntu etc support, but no web support. So unless you are ready for installing the apps everywhere, dont go for it.

9. Obsidian
	No web app, product’s own syncing service needs premium, although you can set up sync with your own services like dropbox, google drive, github, etc but their desktop version is needed for that, as in, the notes go in the desktop dropbox folder/vault and is synced from there, or using the DropSync app for dropbox on android.
	But now after using it with syncthing, ooof oof oof, such smooth experience using it. Although I do have to sync it manually using syncthing but okayz, will keep only longterm notes into this

10. Turtl
	50 MB storage limit on the free plan so cant do much.

11. Notesnook
	Sync issues plenty, but what works works so okay.

13. Crypt.ee
	Feels very slow tbh. Even on PC, otherwise is still a great alternative to notesnook and standard notes
